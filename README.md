## Hi, I'm Will 👋

I'm an electronic engineer who builds AI that runs on real hardware. I've spent five years at Intel in Ireland, first in SoC hardware verification and now on the NPU, the AI engine inside Copilot+ PCs. There I'm the sole developer of the demo platform that runs AI workloads side by side on the NPU, CPU and GPU across up to six machines at once, and I've triaged around 150 third-party AI apps for NPU support.

Outside work I build things, break them and build them again. Most of what I make uses AI as a part, not a gimmick: models are called only where they earn their place, every answer is checked before it's used, and the numbers (cost, tokens, what passed) are measured rather than guessed.

📍 Based in Ireland, in **Melbourne, Australia from 2027**.
